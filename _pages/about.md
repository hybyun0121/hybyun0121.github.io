I am a Ph.D. student in the **Department of Statistics and Data Science** at **Yonsei University**, advised by **[Prof. Kyungwoo Song](https://mlai.yonsei.ac.kr/home)**. I work on **efficient and reliable foundation models**, with a focus on large language models (LLMs): how to make them train more stably, run more efficiently, and remain reliable as data and environments change.

## Research

My research follows two complementary directions:

- **Efficient architectures and stable LLM training.** I design model components and training methods that improve efficiency while preserving capability. My first-author work includes **BHyT (ICML 2026)**, a bounded activation that replaces pre-layer normalization, and **RRD (ICML 2026 Workshop)**, routing-and-residual distillation for converting dense LLMs into sparse mixture-of-experts models.
- **Causal learning and robust generalization.** I study how models can use causal structure to remain reliable under distribution shift and hidden confounding. My first-author work includes **CCL (NeurIPS 2025)**, causal-aware in-context learning for out-of-distribution generalization, and **CBPE (ACM SAC 2026)**, multimodal blood-pressure estimation with latent-confounder modeling.

I am currently extending these interests to **LLM agents that generalize across changing environments**, studying how causal knowledge learned in one environment can support decisions in another. My industry collaborations also include building LLM agents for vehicle fault diagnosis, with a focus on knowledge extraction, memory construction, and retrieval.

See my [publications](/publications/) for the full list of papers and my [CV](/cv/) for more on my research experience.
